Technology Choices

Consent and Permission

When a film crew uses AI to replicate or modify an actor’s face or voice, must they first obtain the actor’s consent? Without a doubt, the answer is yes. While informed consent allows us to legally make actors appear younger or continue unfinished roles, disregarding their wishes constitutes a serious violation of personal rights. As research has shown, most audiences actually find arbitrary face-swapping and reshaping distasteful; what matters most to them is the dignity of an actor’s work and their right to control their own image.

Copyright and Fair Compensation

When AI is deeply involved in film production, issues of copyright and ownership become exceptionally complex. For example, when an actor’s face is used to create a digital double, who actually holds the copyright? As recently noted by the European Parliament’s Research Service (Karttunen, 2026), countries such as Denmark are accelerating legislation to strengthen AI-related protections. Our position is this: Film studios must not use cost reduction and efficiency gains as a pretext to deprive creators and actors of their legitimate earnings.
