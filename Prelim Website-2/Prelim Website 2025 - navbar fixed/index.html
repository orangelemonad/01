Deepfake Technology and Creative Rights

Opportunities, Risks, and Ethical Governance in Cinema

What is Deepfake?

Deepfake technology essentially involves using advanced computer algorithms and neural networks to generate and modify highly lifelike images, videos, and audio.

The Promise in Filmmaking

In cinema, Deepfake technology offers revolutionary opportunities. It brings digital characters to life on screen, helps build magical worlds, and saves a significant amount of production time and money.

The Underlying Risks

To be honest, it also brings its fair share of problems. Critical concerns include using an actor's face and voice without permission or prior notice, spreading misinformation, and potentially putting film and television professionals out of work.

Our Project Goal

We aim to take a closer look at how Deepfakes work and foster a rational discussion about their pros and cons in the film industry. We will examine how this technology sparks artistic creativity while addressing the potential harm it may cause to actors' rights, copyright ownership, and audience trust.
