Chosen Technology / Topic

Deepfake Technology in the Film Industry

Background & Curiosity

When browsing social media, we often come across various funny or extremely realistic 'face-swapping' short videos. At first, we simply thought it was just for fun. But gradually, we became curious: How exactly does the technology work behind the scenes? Later, we discovered that this technology has long been incorporated into the film industry. So we decided to follow the trail and delve deeper into the underlying logic of this technology, as well as how it is currently used in films.

A Double-Edged Sword

To be honest, this technology is definitely a 'double-edged sword'. On the one hand, it brings endless creativity and convenience. But on the other hand, it has also caused many ethical and legal problems. For instance, if a production team uses actors' faces and voices to train the model without permission, can this be considered infringement? What if the technology is misused to create fake news? Even many behind-the-scenes personnel in the film and television industry are worried that this thing might take away their jobs. These are issues that we cannot ignore.

Our Core Focus

Our focus is to explore the opportunities, risks and responsible usage of deepfake technology in the film industry. We will concentrate on the right to informed consent and copyright ownership. It's not about blindly embracing the technology or completely rejecting it. Instead, we want to understand how this technology can enhance the creativity of films while truly protecting the legitimate rights and interests of ordinary people and professionals, and maintaining the trust of the audience.
